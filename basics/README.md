# Basics — the concept articles

Short, plain-language articles on the core concepts of AI work — one
concept per article, written for readers at the start of the
[learning path](../learning-path.md).

Journey articles and the [glossary](../glossary.md) link here instead of
re-explaining: the glossary gives you the two-line definition; a basics
article gives you the whole idea, with an example and the confusions to
avoid.
