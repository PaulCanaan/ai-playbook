# Basics — the concept and tool articles

Short, plain-language articles on the core ideas of AI work — one per
article, written for readers at the start of the
[learning path](../learning-path.md). Two tracks: **concepts** (the ideas)
and **tools** (the specific software the journey runs on).

Journey articles and the [glossary](../glossary.md) link here instead of
re-explaining: the glossary gives you the two-line definition; a basics
article gives you the whole idea, with an example and the confusions to
avoid.
